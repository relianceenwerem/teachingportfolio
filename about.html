Bio

Reliance Enwerem is a doctoral candidate and Fellow (ABD) in the Texts & Technology program at the University of Central Florida. She teaches technical and professional communication with a foundation in digital-humanities methods. Her teaching centers on rehumanizing data: helping students across technical and interdisciplinary fields communicate data about people with care, context, and a feminist ethics of care.

She designs courses, like ENC 3027: Topics in Tech Comm, around a five-dimension heuristic (care, embodiment, contextualization, small data, subjectivity), pairing playful, accessible, UDL-informed teaching with critical AI literacy. Her classrooms treat AI the way they treat data: as something powerful, imperfect, and never neutral, to be interrogated rather than accepted.

Her research interests include transnational digital rhetorics of risk and technical communication, data studies, digital humanities, and feminist theory. She has presented at various conferences and is working on a book project that explores the role of data in shaping contemporary narratives about identity and belonging.
